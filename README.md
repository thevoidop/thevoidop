### 👤 The Human Behind the Screen

I'm a developer who genuinely enjoys building things, but let's be real, half of the time I am figuring out why the thing I built yesterday suddenly decided to stop working today. When I am not writing clean code, I am focused on engineering scalable backend architecture and exploring how machines understand human language.

* 🏗️ **Focus:** Architecting scalable backend solutions and engineering robust systems to solve real-world problems.
* 🤖 **AI / ML Focus:** Working deeply with Natural Language Processing (NLP) to bridge the gap between text and intelligent computation.
* 🤝 **Open to:** Collaborating on impactful open-source projects or talking about distributed systems and NLP.
